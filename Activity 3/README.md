For this activity, I chose breast cancer awareness because I wanted to focus on a health issue that affects many women and to help spread awareness about the importance of early detection. I believe that people should be informed about the possible signs and symptoms, risk factors, and prevention practices because being aware can encourage individuals to take their health seriously and seek medical attention when needed. I chose pink as the main color because it is widely recognized as the color of breast cancer awareness and is associated with hope, support, strength, and solidarity. I used different shades of pink, white, and darker text to make the infographic attractive while keeping the information easy to read. I also added the breast cancer ribbon, illustrations, icons, and statistics to make the message more visual and understandable. I came up with the design by thinking about how I could present important health information in a way that would catch people's attention while still being educational. My main goal was to emphasize that breast cancer awareness matters and that early detection can help save lives.
Project Documentation
For the project documentation, I chose a different real-world healthcare-related issue: cyberattacks on healthcare systems. The topic was chosen because hospitals increasingly depend on digital technology and store sensitive patient information, making them potential targets for cyberattacks. The documentation discusses how these attacks can affect patient privacy, safety, and the delivery of healthcare services, and it proposes stronger cybersecurity measures, staff training, and response plans as solutions. This topic allowed me to connect technology and healthcare, showing that protecting digital systems is also important in protecting patients and ensuring that healthcare services continue safely.
