I learned how AI tools can help me turn my ideas into creative and realistic designs. At first, I wanted to create a matcha café with a cozy and relaxing atmosphere using green and beige colors. However, after seeing the first generated image, I realized that I wanted something more modern, simple, and elegant. This encouraged me to improve my prompt and become more specific about the colors, furniture, lighting, and overall appearance of the café.

I realized that giving clear and detailed instructions is important when using AI because the quality of the result depends on how well I explain my ideas. I also learned that the first result does not always have to be the final one. Sometimes, making changes and exploring other designs can lead to a better outcome. Personally, I preferred the second design because its black, white, and gray color scheme matched my vision of a minimalist matcha café.
