For this activity, I created a five-page cosmetics advertisement for “Mclean Cosmetics” with a makeup collection inspired by beauty, confidence, and self-expression. The designs feature phrases such as “Thank U, Next,” “A New Standard in Everyday Makeup,” “Natural Beauty That Lasts All Day,” and “Kiss & Move On.” I chose lavender/purple as the main color because I wanted the advertisement to have a feminine, elegant, and modern appearance. I combined it with black typography to create strong contrast and make the words stand out, while the red lipstick mark adds a bold accent that connects to makeup and beauty. I came up with the concept by taking inspiration from modern cosmetics advertisements and creating a brand that represents confidence and individuality. I also used different makeup products and beauty images to make each page visually interesting while keeping the same color theme throughout the collection.
