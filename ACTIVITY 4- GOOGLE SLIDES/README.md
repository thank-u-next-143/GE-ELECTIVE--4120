For this group activity, we created a presentation about Google Slides to help our classmates understand what it is, how it works, and how it can be useful in school and other activities. We discussed its history, functions, applications, and the basic steps in creating a presentation. We also included interactive activities and quiz questions because we wanted our discussion to be more engaging rather than just explaining everything. As students, we know that presentations are commonly used for reports and group projects, so we wanted to share information that our classmates could actually apply. While preparing our presentation, we also had the chance to practice our creativity, communication, and teamwork. Overall, this activity helped us become more familiar with Google Slides and taught us the importance of working together to present information in a clear and interesting way.
