*Inspired by Disney's Meet the Robinsons (2007)*

This quote has always been meaningful to me because it reminds me that failure is not the end of the journey. In *Meet the Robinsons*, the message is about learning from mistakes, accepting failures, and continuing to move forward instead of being stuck in the past.

I relate to this because, as a nursing student, there are times when things become stressful and don't go as planned. Sometimes I make mistakes, doubt myself, or feel overwhelmed, but I remind myself that these moments are part of learning and growing.
