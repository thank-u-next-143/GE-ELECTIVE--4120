I created the McLean Cosmetics website because I wanted to combine my love for makeup, beauty, and Ariana Grande with my creativity. I chose the *thank u, next* theme because I love its aesthetic, and I wanted my website to reflect my personal interests. As a student, I also wanted to challenge myself by turning a simple business idea into an attractive and functional online store. Through this activity, I learned that creating a website is not just about making it beautiful but also about making it easy for customers to use. Overall, I created this website because it represents something I genuinely enjoy while allowing me to explore and improve my creativity and digital skills.
