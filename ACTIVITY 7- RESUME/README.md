I created this resume because I wanted to learn how to properly present myself when applying for a job in the future. As a nursing student, I know that having a good resume is important, especially when I start looking for work after graduation. Since I do not have professional nursing experience yet, I focused on my educational background, skills, and experiences during nursing school. For this activity, I also used Gemini and ChatGPT to see how both AI tools could help me organize my information and make my resume look more professional. I noticed that both tools had different ways of presenting the same information, which helped me decide what worked best for me. Through this activity, I realized that creating a resume is not just about listing achievements but also about presenting who I am and what I can offer as a future nurse. Overall, this activity helped me become more prepared and confident about applying for a job someday.
