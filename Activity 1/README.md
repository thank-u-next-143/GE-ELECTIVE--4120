For this activity, I created a promotional poster for Sweetener Café, and I named it after Ariana Grande’s Sweetener album because I really love Ariana Grande and her music. The name “Sweetener” inspired me to create a café concept that feels sweet, warm, and welcoming. I chose brown, beige, black, and white as the main colors because brown represents coffee, while beige gives the design a warm and cozy café feeling. I used black and white for the text and details to create contrast and make the important information easier to read. I also added coffee beans and coffee-related elements to make the theme clear. I came up with the design by combining my love for Ariana Grande with the idea of a simple and inviting coffee shop, making the poster both personal and connected to the activity.
